🎬 Netflix Recommendation System

TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity over 8,790 titles — by Muhammad Abdul Rafay (ML Intern @ Auspify Technologies). Pick a title you love and get instant content-based recommendations — precomputed with scikit-learn, served as a fully static page.

⏳ Loading the recommendation data…

How it works: every title becomes a text profile from its genres, country, director, type and rating. TF-IDF vectorises those profiles and cosine similarity ranks the catalogue by closeness to your pick — pure content-based filtering, no user data.
