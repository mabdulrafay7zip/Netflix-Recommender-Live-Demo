⚙️ How it works

Pure content-based filtering — no accounts, no watch history, no user data.

🧬

1 · Title profiles

Every title becomes a text profile from its genres, country, director, type and rating.

🧮

2 · TF-IDF vectors

Profiles are vectorised with TF-IDF, so distinctive words count more than common ones.

🎯

3 · Cosine ranking

Cosine similarity ranks the whole catalogue by closeness to your pick — the top 8 win.
